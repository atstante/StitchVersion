## Brand & Style

The visual identity embodies an organic-modern sanctuary tailored for expatriates, remote professionals, and global investors seeking premium properties across Southeast Asia. The experience strikes a deliberate balance between warm, sun-drenched tropical hospitality and institutional financial rigor. It eliminates cross-border relocation friction through absolute clarity, serene whitespace, and approachable elegance.

The aesthetic fuses **Modern Organic Warmth** with **Refined Minimalism**:
- **Atmosphere:** Breathable, light-filled, and reassuring. Generous negative space mirrors spacious seaside living and avoids transactional anxiety.
- **Audience:** Discerning expats, digital nomads, and lifestyle investors who demand verified listings, zero hidden fees, and transparent legal parameters.
- **Personality:** Trustworthy, hospitable, effortless, and poised. Visual touchpoints combine grounded botanical depth with energetic amber highlights.

## Colors

The palette balances organic tranquility with high-conversion legibility:

- **Primary (`#086D67`):** Sea-Pine Emerald serves as the institutional foundation. It anchors the primary interactive hierarchy, active navigation tabs, headline price callouts, quiz banners, and key graphical accents. It evokes security, permanence, and lush coastal environments.
- **Secondary (`#F49F1B`):** Sunlit Marigold Amber functions as the primary call-to-action color. It energizes conversion triggers, search buttons, hero CTA points, and decorative headline dashes.
- **Tertiary (`#06524D`):** Deep Rainforest Teal provides rich, grounded hover states, photography overlay gradients, and depth for high-emphasis containers.
- **Neutral (`#1F2937`):** Deep Charcoal Slate provides crisp contrast for body typography and headings in light mode, as well as grounding the institutional footer canvas.
- **Background & Surfaces:** High-purity white (`#FFFFFF`) forms the primary card and page canvas. Muted tones (`#F7F9FC` and `#F8FAFC`) delineate secondary functional bands and dropdown modules without heavy visual friction. Hairline outlines (`#E5E7EB`) deliver neat definition.

## Typography

The type system is powered universally by **Nunito Sans**. Its gently curved terminals offer approachable warmth while maintaining geometric integrity and structural legibility across compact property cards and expansive heroes.

- **Hero & Section Heads:** Large headlines use heavier weights (`800`) with compact line heights (`1.2`–`1.3`). Section titles (`headline-lg`) feature a signature 56px × 4px rounded marigold dash positioned directly beneath the text baseline to establish rhythm.
- **Listing Hierarchy:** Property titles prioritize rapid scanning (`title-card`), paired with uppercase location tags (`label-eyebrow`) in muted charcoal. Financial commitments (`price-lg`) display prominently in Sea-Pine Emerald.
- **Reading Comfort:** Body copy and legal explanations employ an open `1.6` line-height ratio, facilitating effortless comprehension for multi-lingual users reading complex transactional criteria.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout embraces an airy, breathable spatial rhythm anchored by a maximum structural width of `1600px`. Generous vertical spacing prevents visual cognitive overload during high-stakes property searches.

- **Vertical Rhythm:** Main page sections enforce an expansive `space-3xl` (`800px` equivalent / `5rem`) vertical cadence on desktop, tapering to `space-2xl` on tablet and mobile viewports.
- **Card Carousels & Snap Rail:** Featured listings inhabit a horizontal swipe runway using CSS scroll snap (`scroll-snap-type: x mandatory`). On mobile, cards size at `260px` wide; on desktop, they settle at `300px` to `340px` with consistent `space-xl` gaps.
- **Responsive Adaptations:**
  - **Mobile (`<= 600px`):** Single-column content stack, full-width inputs, and collapsed navigation drawers.
  - **Tablet (`601px - 1023px`):** 2-column listing grids, horizontally scrolling showcase ribbons, and clamped 2-line property titles.
  - **Desktop (`>= 1024px`):** Inline horizontal search capsule in sticky navigation, multi-column value proposition grids, and detached next/prev circular pagination arrays.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is expressed through light-filled ambient shadows, subtle boundary strokes, and warm interactive blooms rather than heavy dark drop shadows.

- **Resting Elevation (Level 1):** Header bars, property cards, and feature containers use an ultra-diffused shadow (`0 2px 8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`) combined with a crisp `1px solid #E5E7EB` outline.
- **Floating Overlays & Flyouts (Level 2):** Expanded search panels, contextual currency selectors, and active quiz boxes lift with `0 8px 24px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`.
- **Brand Hover Lift:** Property cards react dynamically on pointer hover by ascending `translateY(-8px)` accompanied by an organic emerald shadow tint (`0 12px 32px rgba(8, 109, 103, 0.15)`), eliminating harsh outlines in favor of tactile elevation.
- **Amber Focus Glow:** Primary conversion triggers (such as hero search actions) project a luminous ambient glow (`0 4px 15px rgba(244, 159, 27, 0.4)`), prioritizing focal hierarchy against photography backgrounds.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a friendly, pill-forward shape scale (level `3`) that conveys safety, organic harmony, and modern hospitality.

- **Capsule / Pill Structures (`999px`):** Applied to the signature header search container, primary CTAs, quick filter tags, category pills, and circular pagination controls.
- **Card & Banner Containers (`rounded-lg` / `2rem` equivalent):** Property cards and interactive feature highlights utilize `20px` to `24px` continuous radii, ensuring photo framing feels natural and soft.
- **Compact Controls (`rounded` / `1rem` equivalent):** Interactive input fields, drop-down sheets, and accordion containers feature refined `12px` to `16px` boundaries.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Hero Button:** Solid `#F49F1B` surface, high-contrast white text, `12px` border radius, bold typography, elevated by an amber ambient shadow. Shifts to `#D68912` and elevates `translateY(-3px)` on hover.
- **Search Pill Action:** Full pill format (`border-radius: 999px`), `10px 26px` padding, bright amber background with crisp white typography.
- **Secondary Ghost Action:** Transparent surface encased by a `2px solid #086D67` border and emerald label. Inverts smoothly to solid `#086D67` fill on pointer entry.
- **Pagination Directionals:** Symmetrical circular pills (`46px x 46px`) in clean white with `#086D67` arrow glyphs, shifting to solid emerald on hover.

### Header Search Capsule
- Encased in a single floating pill container (`border-radius: 999px`) bound by a hairline stroke (`#E5E7EB`).
- Integrates a flexible location text field, a hairline vertical divider, a deal-type trigger ('Rent' / 'Buy') with an animated rotational chevron, and an inline amber search button.

### Real Estate Listing Cards
- Built with a `20px` corner radius, pure white container, and masked top visual frame (`220px` height) with smooth image zoom (`scale(1.05)`) on hover.
- Content compartment features `20px` internal padding: uppercase grey location eyebrow, truncated two-line title in dark charcoal slate, and a thin top-bordered metadata bar separating area/room stats from an emerald price indicator (`$450 / mo`).

### Chips & Filter Badges
- Pill-shaped tags (`border-radius: 999px`) with `6px 14px` padding.
- Default state: Soft mist background (`#F7F9FC`) with subtle `#E5E7EB` outline.
- Active state: Sea-Pine Emerald fill (`#086D67`) with crisp white typography.

### Input Fields & Selects
- Form inputs feature soft `12px` radii, `12px 16px` padding, and `#1F2937` font colors.
- Resting state features a clean `#E5E7EB` border. Focus states transition seamlessly to a 2px `#086D67` stroke without harsh default browser rings.

### Trust & E-E-A-T Value Blocks
- Modular feature cards with clean white surfaces, `16px` border radii, and `24px` internal padding.
- Emphasizes transparency through bold emerald headlines detailing verified contracts, escrow protections, and localized expatriate advisory support.
