## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a serene, modern, and deeply trustworthy digital sanctuary tailored for expatriates, digital nomads, and global investors seeking premium residential properties across Southeast Asia. The visual atmosphere marries the relaxed elegance of tropical resort living with the rigorous transparency, structural authority, and E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness) criteria expected of a premier international brokerage.

Drawing from a refined blend of modern corporate clarity and sun-drenched hospitality minimalism, the design avoids harsh borders or noisy gradients. Instead, it relies on generous negative space, gentle ambient depth, pristine white surfaces, and tranquil emerald and amber accents. The resulting experience feels light, unhurried, and welcoming—transforming complex cross-border transactions into an intuitive, secure journey.

## Colors

The color palette establishes an architectural harmony between deep equatorial earthiness and radiant sunlit hospitality:

- **Primary (`#086D67`)**: Deep emerald teal anchors the brand foundation. It symbolizes safety, lush regional landscapes, and financial stability. Applied across price leads, primary headings, key icon graphics, and brand assets.
- **Secondary (`#F49F1B`)**: Sunlit warm amber serves as the focal interaction driver. Reserved strictly for primary action buttons, search pills, interactive highlights, and decorative rule markers to provide unmistakable visual hierarchy.
- **Tertiary (`#1F2937`)**: Deep slate charcoal brings grounding contrast to typographic elements and prominent anchor structures such as the dark footer.
- **Neutral (`#6B7280`)**: Mineral gray handles contextual metadata, supporting subtitles, architectural dimension callouts, and quiet iconography.

Surfaces default to clean white (`#FFFFFF`) layered against soft cloud mist (`#F7F9FC`) for alternating visual rhythm. Subtle border hairlines (`#E5E7EB`) provide light containment without heavy visual bulk.

## Typography

The design system standardizes on **Nunito Sans** for all textual hierarchies. Its rounded terminals and open apertures humanize transactional property negotiations while preserving readability across property specs, pricing grids, and legal notices.

### Typographic Hierarchy
- **Display & Section Titles**: Formatted with ExtraBold (`800`) weight. Section titles incorporate a slight negative tracking (`-0.5px`) for optical punch, complemented by a signature amber decorative accent rule (`56px × 4px`).
- **Feature & Card Titles**: Bold (`700`) weight ensures quick scanning over multi-column and scroll-snap arrangements.
- **Editorial Copy & Guidance**: Uses Regular (`400`) and Medium (`500`) weights with a comfortable `1.6` line-height multiple to ensure long-form listings remain readable under varied ambient lighting.
- **Metadata & Badges**: Captions and property tags feature SemiBold (`600`) casing paired with a loose `0.05em` letter spacing to balance the soft geometric contours of the font.

## Layout & Spacing

A fluid grid system is anchored by a centered container with a maximum width of `1600px`. Spacing is calculated on a steady 4px/8px modular scale to maintain consistent rhythmic intervals:

### Layout Rhythm
- **Canvas Margins**: Set to `1.25rem` (20px) on mobile viewports, expanding to `2.5rem` on tablets, and reaching up to `5rem` (80px) on wide desktop displays to preserve airy negative space.
- **Section Breaks**: Standard vertical padding between thematic blocks is `5rem` (80px), reducing to `2.5rem` (40px) for compact transitional banners and quiz callouts.
- **Horizontal Carousels**: Listing sections implement native scroll-snap tracks (`scroll-snap-type: x mandatory`) with an item-to-item gap of `1.5rem` (24px) and edge scroll padding matching the canvas margins.

### Responsive Breakpoints
- **Mobile (`≤600px`)**: Single-column vertical layout. Hero padding compresses to `64px 20px`. Property cards shrink to a fixed flex-basis of `260px` within carousel viewports.
- **Tablet (`601px - 1023px`)**: 2-column or fluid grid flow. The search pill shifts into an accessible stacked card format.
- **Desktop (`≥1024px`)**: Full multi-column layout, horizontal capsule navigation, and auto-fit feature grids (`minmax(280px, 1fr)`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses clean tonal surfaces paired with diffused, ambient shadows to evoke a sense of physical weightlessness without visual noise:

- **Baseline Rest (`elevation-sm`)**: Applied to cards, sticky headers, and input modules. Rendered as `0 2px 8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`. It lifts elements gently off soft cloud mist backgrounds while maintaining crisp edges with a 1px border (`#E5E7EB`).
- **Interactive Elevated (`elevation-md`)**: Applied to flyouts, floating select popovers, and elevated promo banners. Uses `0 8px 24px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`.
- **Primary CTA Radiance**: Amber action triggers employ an intentional luminous warm bloom (`0 4px 15px rgba(244, 159, 27, 0.40)`), creating a tactile, clickable visual presence.
- **Hover Bloom (`elevation-hover`)**: Hovered property cards translate upward (`translateY(-8px)`) while expanding their shadow into an emerald glow (`0 12px 32px rgba(8, 109, 103, 0.15)`), confirming cursor focus through the primary brand hue.
- **Tonal Layering**: Deep hero photographic visuals use an emerald-tinted directional gradient overlay (`rgba(8, 109, 103, 0.70)` to `rgba(8, 109, 103, 0.85)`), guaranteeing AAA contrast ratios for headline text.

## Shapes

The design system implements a rounded shape language (Level 2), standardizing base UI elements at `0.5rem` (8px), container cards at `1rem` to `1.25rem` (16px–20px), and navigation elements as circular or pill-shaped capsules:

- **Buttons & Controls**: Primary actions and form inputs utilize smooth `12px` corner radii (`rounded-md`), balancing friendly approachability with structured geometric boundaries.
- **Cards & Banners**: Property containers and interactive banners employ `20px` radii (`rounded-xl`), creating soft frames for architectural imagery.
- **Search Capsules & Badges**: Quick-search bars and category status tags adopt a `100px` pill geometry (`rounded-full`), reinforcing effortless fluidity.
- **Carousel Controls**: Directional arrow controls are perfect circles (`50%` radius), offering clear spatial targets.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: Solid warm amber background (`#F49F1B`), bold white label, `12px` border radius, `16px 44px` padding, and an amber luminous glow shadow. On hover, background deepens to `#D68912` accompanied by a `-3px` vertical translation.
- **Search Action Pill**: Solid amber background, `100px` pill radius, `10px 26px` padding, with crisp white typography.
- **Secondary Outline**: Transparent fill framed with a `2px solid #086D67` border and primary emerald text. Shifts to solid emerald fill with white text upon cursor hover.
- **Carousel Nav Controls**: Circular (`46px × 46px`) buttons on a clean white surface with a `1px solid #E5E7EB` border. Inactive/disabled states rest at `0.35` opacity without hover translation.

### Property Showcase Cards
- **Structure**: Encased in a `20px` rounded container with `1px solid #E5E7EB`, pure white background, and rest shadow (`0 2px 8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`).
- **Media Header**: 220px fixed-height photo area with smooth zoom transition (`transform 0.5s ease; scale(1.05)`) on hover.
- **Body Area**: `20px` inner padding. Displays uppercase location metadata (`13px`, `#6B7280`), followed by a bold card headline (`18px`, `#1F2937`).
- **Footer Division**: A hairline horizontal stroke (`1px solid #E5E7EB`) separates property details from price blocks. Displays prices in prominent `22px` ExtraBold emerald teal (`#086D67`), with specification items (bedrooms, bathrooms, area) justified along the bottom.

### Quick-Search Capsule
- **Desktop**: A floating pill container (`100px` radius) with `5px` outer padding and a subtle `1px solid #E5E7EB` outline. Contains segmented input slots separated by `1px × 26px` vertical hairlines.
- **Mobile / Tablet**: Morphs adaptively into a stacked form card with `12px` corner rounding, full-width field containers, and an anchored full-width amber submit trigger.

### Input Fields & Select Dropdowns
- **Fields**: Neutral surface background (`#FFFFFF` or `#F7F9FC`), subtle `1px solid #E5E7EB` border, `12px` corner radius, and `12px 16px` inset padding. Focus states introduce an emerald outline ring (`#086D67`) with zero layout shift.
- **Dropdown Menus**: Absolute floating white cards (`12px` radius) supported by medium elevation (`0 8px 24px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`). Active selected options take a primary emerald fill with white text; hovered options take a soft cloud mist fill (`#F7F9FC`).

### Accordions & E-E-A-T Trust Modules
- **Trust Modules**: Clean cards framed with `12px` radii and `30px` padding, featuring primary teal headers that highlight direct pricing, local bilingual support, and verified land titles.
- **FAQ Accordion**: Contained rows with `12px` radii and `#F7F9FC` background headers. Smooth expansion transitions reveal body text below a crisp `1px solid #E5E7EB` divider line. Rotating emerald indicators mark open/closed states.

### Status Chips & Tags
- **Style**: Compact pill-shaped badges (`100px` radius) with `4px 12px` padding. Employs soft emerald tinted backgrounds (`rgba(8, 109, 103, 0.10)`) with bold emerald text (`#086D67`) for status verification tags.
